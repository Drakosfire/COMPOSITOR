# Local ingestion experiment index

This index follows the [ecosystem hardware research guide](https://github.com/Drakosfire/DungeonOverMind/blob/308700f19be93963b2d4d7598137ce9154e2ecf7/Docs/Guides/GUIDE-hardware-research-records.md). Local-model ingestion and retrieval experiments use one immutable identifier per bounded comparison. A record begins as a pre-registered plan, then appends exact activation, first-result, review, and scoring receipts. Preserve failed and interrupted runs. Private inputs and outputs remain outside Git; public records contain source-safe identities and limitations.

- [LOCAL-INGEST-001](LOCAL-INGEST-001.md) — **FOUR-CASE PROVIDED-EVIDENCE PILOT RECORDED**. The measured addendum records two 2024/5.5 PHB occupancy cases and two Conks narrative cases against a frozen pilot reference: eight local Gemma generation/typed-selection calls completed, all framed/schema-valid; one of four strict joint cases passed. This is failure-finding evidence on supplied text, not an accuracy estimate or end-to-end ingestion/consumer result. Zero provider calls, retries, timeouts, or backend failures occurred. Prior plans, preflights, outputs, and gold are preserved; Jev and any successor treatment remain separately gated.

The [accepted Composition refinement](../Design/CONTRACT-composition.md) establishes golden reconstruction and functional witnesses as the target for future model-stack comparisons. The [next complete-section unit](../Roadmaps/ROADMAP-composition-pr-stack.md#next-proposed-golden-unit) is proposed, not activated; it uses prepared text/images with OCR out of scope. Development on the pilot's exposed cases must not be reported as a new holdout or rescore of its original result.

Each record must pin the question and hypotheses before a run; source and dataset hashes; document-held-out policy; gold author, independent source critique, human seed review, and freeze status; backend, model bytes, quantization, prompt/settings; metric denominators; elapsed, queue, GPU memory/usage, reviewer time and monetary cost; failures; reproducible commands and durable artifact locations; independent review; and limitations. A blank field means **unmeasured**, not zero.
