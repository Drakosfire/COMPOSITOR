## Tight Jev decision-loop hypothesis — inactive

PRIME proposed exploring a narrow review loop for claims whose evidence is already present but whose **typed relationship or uncertainty** is missing: identity/form (005), charge timing and authored-ending tension (039, 043), and possibly scene/grey-box typing (002). Candidate discovery should be gold-blind across the whole package, with exact source quote, physical page, resource IDs, and the proposed relation or diagnostic. Each review asks one bounded choice with an explicit **unresolved** option. Jev output is limited to a typed choice or uncertainty with its receipt; the harness records the pinned source basis, and a human reviewer supplies the rationale. Deterministic validation and human review precede any additive immutable package revision. Re-adjudicate all 48 cases for regressions and measure candidate-generation, decision, and reviewer effort separately. The loop must add typed decisions rather than replacement prose or unverified World truth.

The printed statblock conflict (013) should be **detected deterministically** by comparing printed values; a review loop may route its disposition but must preserve both original values. OCR omissions and corruptions (026, 027, 038), map meaning (022), and edition-specific rule bindings (012, 034) lack evidence or authority that a narrow choice can create. They retain the owner/source/rule gates above. Jev would be an **additional model experiment**; this design hypothesis is **not permission to call Jev**. The current DeepSeek OCR 2-only restriction remains in force until the operator and PRIME separately authorize a model experiment with exact scope and caps.

## Proposed execution and comparison

1. **Owner recovery decision.** PRIME pins the current RulesIngestion ref and reviews the three existing private OCR repros before assigning an owner correction. Any new owner artifact is immutable, with source PDF/page, recipe, output, gate, model-call, and local-runtime receipt pins. A deterministic correction has a zero-provider budget; a new OCR2 inference requires a separate finite lease. Failed gates and original bad text remain inspectable.
2. **COMPOSITOR assembly treatment.** On a new isolated branch, pin the exact owner input revision and a generalizable semantic recipe. Produce a *new* private package revision with source-quoted identity, conflict, ambiguity, and reviewed asset-link candidates. Keep the unedited first output of that recipe and later human-accepted repairs separate. Do not hardcode Sheep names, case IDs, or expected answers into a reusable extractor. Synthetic public fixtures exercise each primitive; the real package and review decisions stay private.
3. **Frozen-input comparison.** Reopen the same exact PDF, gold, freeze record, and baseline packages before either treatment. Apply the original 48-case rubric to **all 48 cases**, including the 37 earlier passes, to detect regressions; report the two treatment results side by side by source fidelity, identity/relationship quality, audience, rule binding, and task readiness. Keep the seven owner integration cases unexecuted until their real runtimes are pinned. Gold-informed repair is labeled a development treatment and cannot be reported as an independent first-ingestion success. A later untouched source or held-out cases test transfer.
4. **Cost and effort receipt.** Use the private experiment ledger or an equivalently hash-pinned private receipt to record source and recipe versions, owner artifact hashes, immutable first and repaired outputs, provider/local model invocations, retries, actual or conservatively reserved spend, wall time, code/recipe engineering time, page inspection, per-case review and manual correction time, and unresolved work. Report per-case fixes and regressions separately. Do not infer zero total compute cost from zero external provider spend.

Before implementation, PRIME names the exact owner ref, repository write paths, data handling basis, source/page scope, expected witness, private output and ledger location, and separate call/round/spend caps if inference is used.
