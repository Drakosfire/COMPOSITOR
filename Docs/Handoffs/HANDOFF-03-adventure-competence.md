# Draft handoff 03 — adventure competence

Status: **proposed, unassigned**. Roadmap: [Composition PR stack](../Roadmaps/ROADMAP-composition-pr-stack.md). Depends on a pinned document-to-draft route and private benchmark readiness.

**Current sequencing:** begin with the roadmap's proposed complete-section golden Composition (text, relationships, and an image), then grow coverage into the separate one-shot suites. Prepared corpus/evidence is available; OCR selection, scoring, and reruns are out of scope for this next experiment. Keep historical OCR2 first results and observed defects intact.

**Pin at dispatch:** exact local revisions and handling permissions for *Of Conks & Cons* and *A Wild Sheep Chase*; separate frozen gold versions under the [benchmark protocol](../Design/BENCHMARKS-one-shots.md); recipe/model versions; isolated private store; provider data, call/round and spend limits. The two gold authors and parent source review are preparation, not assumed complete.

**Deliver:** one recorded interpretation/assembly recipe that turns each adventure's evidence into resources and relationships, including procedures, possible outcomes, mechanics references, and assets. Use existing GenerationEngine execution where its contract fits. Preserve each unedited first result, validation findings, later repairs, and experiment receipts separately. Keep the public PR source-safe.

**Prove:** freeze and assess both golden witnesses: reconstruction of content/meaning/organization with bidirectional source coverage, and named consumer tasks with fixed starting state, selection, audience, dependencies, completion checks, and acceptable alternatives. Include source/adaptation/dependency cases and an image with exact bytes/provenance; image descriptions are distinct resources. Report source fidelity, omissions, identity/relationship quality, audience handling, and practical Worldbuilding, Planning, and Playing outcomes separately for each adventure. Show unresolved material and actual repair effort. A passing tiny fixture is insufficient; neither adventure's score may hide the other's failure.

**Boundary:** private benchmark runs are authorized only by a specific execution scope. This bite does not grant full corpus ingestion, public redistribution of source-derived gold, or World publication. Numerical acceptance thresholds must be frozen before scored runs, not chosen from results.

**Hand onward:** pinned recipe, per-adventure baseline and limitations, failure examples safe to share, and package artifacts or private locators usable for context integration. If one-shot source readiness blocks scoring, report a partial witness and replan rather than substitute another source.
